Computer modeling of acrylic acid–choline chloride deep eutectic solvent associates using density functional theory.
The objective of this thesis is to perform quantum-chemical modeling—using density functional theory—of an associate formed by acrylic acid and choline chloride, in order to establish a correlation between its structure, the energetics of its intermolecular interactions, and its propensity to form a deep eutectic solvent.
To achieve this objective, the following tasks must be addressed:
1. Optimize the geometry of the individual molecules (acrylic acid and choline chloride) as well as their complexes with varying stoichiometries. 
2. Calculate the intermolecular interaction energies and hydrogen-bonding parameters for the complexes under study.
